CAR INSURANCE COMPARISON GUIDE

Compare car insurance on equal terms.

Car-insurance prices only make sense when the policies being compared protect you in similar ways. A premium that looks cheaper can come with lower liability limits, a higher deductible, fewer optional protections or different eligibility rules. The first step is to make the comparison as close to apples-to-apples as possible.

Match the coverage first

Start with the same liability limits and the same comprehensive and collision deductibles. If one option includes uninsured-motorist coverage, rental reimbursement or roadside assistance and another does not, note the difference before comparing the premium. State requirements vary, so minimum legal coverage is not the same thing as an individualized recommendation.

Know what kind of price you are seeing

A live carrier or licensed-partner quote is different from a rough estimate or marketing example. AutoRateFinder is currently a public comparison preview and does not present invented carrier prices as live quotes. Live quote connections remain pending.

Review the full cost

Look beyond the monthly figure. Check payment-plan fees, installment charges, policy terms, discounts, deductibles and the amount you would need to pay after a covered claim. A lower premium can still create a larger out-of-pocket burden if the deductible is substantially higher.

Check discount eligibility

Carriers may offer discounts for factors such as multiple vehicles, bundling home and auto, low mileage, telematics participation, good-student status, defensive-driving courses, anti-theft equipment or paying in full. Availability and eligibility vary by carrier and state, and only the carrier can confirm a discount.

Verify before switching

If you decide to change insurers, verify the final policy terms and effective date with the licensed provider. Avoid cancelling an existing policy until replacement coverage is active and confirmed. Questions about your individual insurance needs should be handled by an appropriately licensed professional.
