PRIVACY NOTICE

Your quote details and payment

Updated September 25, 2026

To prepare a paid educational report, AutoRateFinder receives the state, ZIP, car year/make/model, premiums, deductibles and coverage choices you enter. We store this information with a random order ID in our access-controlled database. We do not ask for your name, phone number, driver's-license number or Social Security number in this form.

Payments

Stripe hosts the checkout and collects payment and contact details under its own privacy notice. Our system stores a Checkout Session ID and payment status to unlock the report. Card numbers do not reach AutoRateFinder.

Report access

Your browser stores a random access token so you can return to the paid report. We store only its cryptographic hash on the server. Someone using the same browser may be able to open your report. Save or print it if you want a copy. Clearing browser storage may remove convenient access; contact us with your Stripe payment receipt if you need help.

Use and sharing

We use the form details to create your report and handle payment or support requests. This purchase does not send your quote details to insurers or licensed partners. We do not sell these details as leads. Analytics should not contain your quote figures.

Security and deletion

Transport is encrypted, the order database is not open to public queries, and the reporting function checks payment and a private access token. To request access to or deletion of your report details, email autoratefinderpartnerships@gmail.com with enough purchase information to identify the order. Payment and tax records may need to be retained separately.
