    Why a renewal can change even when you did not change cars

    The National Association of Insurance Commissioners says auto pricing can use factors such as location, driving record, claims history, vehicle type, vehicle use, miles driven and the coverage or deductible selected. Which factors are allowed can vary by state.

    That means a national insurance trend does not necessarily describe your own renewal. Your policy is priced using your insurer's rating plan and the information tied to your vehicle, household and location.

    Frequently asked questions

    Why did my car insurance renewal go up?

    A renewal can change because of rating factors such as location, driving record, claims history, vehicle type, mileage, chosen coverage, deductibles, discounts and insurer pricing changes. State rules affect which factors can be used.

    Does a higher renewal mean my coverage improved?

    Not necessarily. Compare the term, limits, deductibles and optional coverages before assuming the new policy is otherwise identical.
