FAQ

What drivers usually want to know.

Does AutoRateFinder sell insurance?

No. AutoRateFinder is being built as an insurance comparison and referral platform, not an insurance carrier or agency. Final quotes and policies are provided by licensed insurers or licensed partners.

Will I always see the absolute cheapest insurer in the U.S.?

No comparison service can guarantee access to every insurer. Results depend on partner availability, state, driver profile and the data supplied. We will clearly identify the providers included in a comparison.

Why should I compare the same coverage limits?

A lower premium can simply mean lower limits or a higher deductible. AutoRateFinder is designed to help compare similar coverage so price differences are more meaningful.

Do I have to buy a policy after comparing?

No. Comparing is free and there is no obligation to purchase through a partner.

Can rates change after I click through?

Yes. Final pricing is determined by the insurer after it verifies the information needed for a quote or application. Rates and eligibility can change based on underwriting information.

How does AutoRateFinder make money?

AutoRateFinder may receive compensation from licensed partners when a user clicks, requests a quote or purchases a policy. We will disclose partner relationships and do not charge drivers a comparison fee.
