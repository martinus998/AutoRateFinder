COMING WITH SECURE ACCOUNTS
FAQ

Clear answers before you share anything.

Does AutoRateFinder sell insurance?

No. AutoRateFinder is a comparison/referral technology project, not an insurance carrier or licensed insurance producer. Final quotes and policies must come from appropriately licensed insurers, agencies or producers.

Are the prices on AutoRateFinder real live quotes?

Not yet. Live carrier connections are pending. We do not invent insurer prices. Profile insights in preview mode are educational and are labeled as not being quotes.

Do I have to enter my phone number?

No phone number is required to start the preview comparison. A future licensed partner may require contact details to produce or bind a quote, and that handoff must be disclosed before submission.

Will you show every insurer in America?

No comparison service can guarantee every carrier. Results will depend on partner availability, state and eligibility. We plan to show which providers are included rather than implying the whole market was searched.

Why can a final price change?

Insurers may verify driving history, claims, prior insurance, vehicle data and other permitted underwriting information. We will flag that distinction before a user leaves AutoRateFinder.

How does AutoRateFinder make money?

AutoRateFinder may receive referral or transaction compensation from licensed partners. Sponsored placement, if used, will be labeled. Drivers are not charged a comparison fee.
